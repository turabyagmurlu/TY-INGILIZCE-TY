
##### LEVEL 1 A1 Seviye 1 — Başlangıç

=== 1-1 [vocab] Selamlaşma
desc: Günlük selamlaşma ifadeleri.
  1. Hello = Merhaba | Hello, how are you? | Merhaba, nasılsın?
  2. Good morning = Günaydın | Good morning, mom. | Günaydın, anne.
  3. Good evening = İyi akşamlar | Good evening! | İyi akşamlar!
  4. Goodbye = Hoşça kal | Goodbye, see you. | Hoşça kal, görüşürüz.
  5. How are you? = Nasılsın? | Hello, how are you? | Merhaba, nasılsın?
  6. I am fine = İyiyim | I am fine, thank you. | İyiyim, teşekkürler.
  7. Hi = Selam | Hi, Ayşe! | Selam, Ayşe!
  8. Good afternoon = İyi öğleden sonralar | Good afternoon, sir. | İyi öğleden sonralar, efendim.
  9. Good night = İyi geceler | Good night, sleep well. | İyi geceler, iyi uykular.
  10. Welcome = Hoş geldiniz | Welcome to our home! | Evimize hoş geldiniz!
  11. And you? = Ya sen? | I am fine. And you? | İyiyim. Ya sen?
info: İngilizce'de 'Hello' her zaman kullanılır. 'Good morning' sabah, 'Good evening' akşam içindir. 'How are you?' hal hatır sormaktır; kısa cevabı 'I am fine' (İyiyim).

=== 1-2 [grammar] Gramer: am / is / are (olmak)
intro: İngilizcede "olmak" fiili üç şekilde kullanılır: am, is, are. Türkçedeki "-im, -sin, -dir" eklerine benzer.
rule: <b>I</b> ile <b>am</b>, <b>he/she/it</b> ile <b>is</b>, <b>you/we/they</b> ile <b>are</b> kullanılır. Örnek: "I <b>am</b> happy" = "Mutlu<b>yum</b>".
structure: [["Özne","Fiil","Örnek"],["I (ben)","am","I am a teacher (öğretmenim)"],["You (sen)","are","You are kind (naziksin)"],["He/She/It (o)","is","She is happy (o mutlu)"],["We/They (biz/onlar)","are","We are home (evdeyiz)"]]
  ex: I am Deniz. = Ben Deniz'im.
  ex: You are my friend. = Sen benim arkadaşımsın.
  ex: He is tall. = O uzun boylu.
  ex: We are ready. = Biz hazırız.
  ex: They are here. = Onlar burada.
  Q1: I ___ happy. [am / is / are] -> am :: I ile her zaman 'am' kullanılır.
  Q2: She ___ a doctor. [is / am / are] -> is :: He/She/It ile 'is' kullanılır.
  Q3: They ___ my friends. [are / is / am] -> are :: They ile 'are' kullanılır.
  Q4: We ___ at home. [are / is / am] -> are :: We ile 'are' kullanılır.
  Q5: He ___ tired. [is / are / am] -> is :: He ile 'is' kullanılır.
  Q6: You ___ my teacher. [are / is / am] -> are :: You ile 'are' kullanılır.
  Q7: It ___ cold today. [is / are / am] -> is :: It ile 'is' kullanılır.
  Q8: My mother ___ at home. [is / are / am] -> is :: 'My mother' = she (o) → 'is'.

=== 1-4 [grammar] Gramer: Çoğullar (-s)
intro: Bir şeyden birden fazla olduğunda kelimenin sonuna genellikle -s eklenir.
rule: Çoğul için sona <b>-s</b> ekle: cat → cat<b>s</b>. Kelime <b>-s, -x, -ch, -sh</b> ile bitiyorsa <b>-es</b> ekle: box → box<b>es</b>.
structure: [["Tekil","Çoğul"],["cat (kedi)","cats"],["book (kitap)","books"],["car (araba)","cars"],["box (kutu)","boxes"],["bus (otobüs)","buses"]]
  ex: two cats = iki kedi
  ex: three books = üç kitap
  ex: five apples = beş elma
  ex: many boxes = birçok kutu
  Q1: one car, two ___ [cars / car / cares] -> cars :: car → cars (sadece -s).
  Q2: one box, two ___ [boxes / boxs / box] -> boxes :: -x ile bitince -es: box → boxes.
  Q3: I have three ___ (book). [books / book / bookes] -> books :: book → books.
  Q4: one bus, two ___ [buses / buss / bus] -> buses :: -s ile bitince -es: bus → buses.
  Q5: one apple, two ___ [apples / apple / applees] -> apples :: apple → apples (sadece -s).
  Q6: one watch, two ___ [watches / watchs / watch] -> watches :: -ch ile bitince -es: watch → watches.
  Q7: one dish, three ___ [dishes / dishs / dish] -> dishes :: -sh ile bitince -es: dish → dishes.

=== 1-6 [grammar] Gramer: a / an
intro: Tekil bir şeyden "bir tane" derken ismin önüne a veya an koyarız.
rule: Kelime <b>sessiz bir sesle</b> başlıyorsa <b>a</b>: a book. <b>Sesli bir sesle</b> (a, e, i, o, u sesi) başlıyorsa <b>an</b>: an apple. Önemli olan yazılış değil, okunuştur: <b>an hour</b> (h okunmaz, "aur"), <b>a university</b> ("yu" sesiyle başlar).
structure: [["a (sessiz)","an (sesli)"],["a car","an apple"],["a dog","an egg"],["a house","an orange"]]
  ex: a book = bir kitap
  ex: an apple = bir elma
  ex: a dog = bir köpek
  ex: an egg = bir yumurta
  Q1: ___ apple [an / a] -> an :: apple sesli bir sesle (a) başlar → an.
  Q2: ___ book [a / an] -> a :: book sessiz bir sesle (b) başlar → a.
  Q3: ___ egg [an / a] -> an :: egg sesli bir sesle (e) başlar → an.
  Q4: ___ house [a / an] -> a :: house'da h okunur, sessiz sesle başlar → a.
  Q5: ___ orange [an / a] -> an :: orange sesli bir sesle (o) başlar → an.
  Q6: ___ car [a / an] -> a :: car sessiz bir sesle (k) başlar → a.
  Q7: ___ hour [an / a] -> an :: hour'da h okunmaz, "aur" diye sesli başlar → an.
  Q8: ___ university [a / an] -> a :: university "yu" sesiyle başlar → a.

=== 1-8 [grammar] Gramer: this / that / these / those
intro: Bir şeyi gösterirken yakın mı uzak mı, tekil mi çoğul mu olduğuna göre kelime değişir.
rule: <b>this</b> = bu (yakın, tekil), <b>that</b> = şu/o (uzak, tekil), <b>these</b> = bunlar (yakın, çoğul), <b>those</b> = şunlar/onlar (uzak, çoğul).
structure: [["","Tekil","Çoğul"],["Yakın","this","these"],["Uzak","that","those"]]
  ex: This is my phone. = Bu benim telefonum.
  ex: That is your car. = Şu senin araban.
  ex: These are books. = Bunlar kitap.
  ex: Those are cats. = Şunlar kedi.
  Q1: ___ is my book. (yakın, tekil) [This / These / Those] -> This :: Yakın + tekil = this.
  Q2: ___ are my friends. (yakın, çoğul) [These / This / That] -> These :: Yakın + çoğul = these.
  Q3: ___ is his house. (uzak, tekil) [That / This / Those] -> That :: Uzak + tekil = that.
  Q4: ___ are her shoes. (uzak, çoğul) [Those / These / That] -> Those :: Uzak + çoğul = those.
  Q5: Look at ___ bird in the sky! (uzak, tekil) [that / these / those] -> that :: Uzak + tekil = that.
  Q6: ___ apples here are fresh. (yakın, çoğul) [These / That / This] -> These :: Yakın + çoğul = these.
  Q7: Is ___ your bag? (yakın, tekil) [this / those / these] -> this :: Yakın + tekil = this.

=== 1-12 [grammar] Gramer: my / your / his / her (iyelik)
intro: Bir şeyin kime ait olduğunu söylerken ismin önüne küçük bir kelime koyarız. Türkçedeki "-im, -in, -i" eklerine benzer.
rule: <b>I → my</b> (benim), <b>you → your</b> (senin), <b>he → his</b> (onun, erkek), <b>she → her</b> (onun, kadın), <b>it → its</b> (onun, eşya/hayvan), <b>we → our</b> (bizim), <b>they → their</b> (onların). Örnek: "<b>my</b> house" = "ev<b>im</b>".
structure: [["Özne","İyelik","Örnek"],["I","my","my name (adım)"],["you","your","your bag (çantan)"],["he","his","his car (onun arabası)"],["she","her","her son (onun oğlu)"],["we","our","our house (evimiz)"],["they","their","their children (çocukları)"]]
  ex: My name is Ayşe. = Benim adım Ayşe.
  ex: Is this your phone? = Bu senin telefonun mu?
  ex: His wife is a nurse. = Onun eşi hemşire.
  ex: Her daughter lives in London. = Onun kızı Londra'da yaşıyor.
  ex: Our house is small. = Evimiz küçük.
  Q1: I have a cat. ___ cat is white. [My / Your / Her] -> My :: I → my (benim).
  Q2: Ali has a car. ___ car is red. [His / Her / Their] -> His :: Ali erkek → his (onun).
  Q3: Ayşe is here. ___ son is a doctor. [Her / His / Our] -> Her :: Ayşe kadın → her (onun).
  Q4: We have a garden. ___ garden is big. [Our / Their / My] -> Our :: we → our (bizim).
  Q5: They live here. ___ house is old. [Their / Our / His] -> Their :: they → their (onların).
  Q6: What is ___ name? (senin) [your / you / my] -> your :: Senin = your. 'you' öznedir, 'your' iyeliktir.
  Q7: My husband and I love ___ children. [our / their / his] -> our :: My husband and I = we → our.

##### LEVEL 2 A1+ Seviye 2 — Temel

=== 2-1 [grammar] Gramer: Geniş Zaman (Present Simple)
intro: Her zaman yaptığımız, alışkanlık olan işleri anlatır. "Her gün çay içerim" gibi.
rule: I/You/We/They + <b>fiilin yalın hali</b>: I drink. He/She/It + <b>fiil + s</b>: He drink<b>s</b>. (-o, -s, -sh, -ch, -x ile bitenlere <b>-es</b>: go → goes, watch → watches. Sessiz harf + y → <b>-ies</b>: study → studies. have → has.)
structure: [["Özne","Fiil","Örnek"],["I / You / We / They","work","I work every day"],["He / She / It","works","She works here"],["He / She / It","goes","He goes to school"]]
  ex: I drink tea. = Çay içerim.
  ex: She drinks coffee. = O kahve içer.
  ex: We play every day. = Her gün oynarız.
  ex: He goes to work. = O işe gider.
  Q1: She ___ tea. (drink) [drinks / drink / drinking] -> drinks :: She ile fiile -s: drinks.
  Q2: I ___ every day. (work) [work / works / working] -> work :: I ile fiil yalın: work.
  Q3: He ___ to school. (go) [goes / gos / go] -> goes :: -o ile bitince -es: go → goes.
  Q4: They ___ football. (play) [play / plays / playing] -> play :: They ile fiil yalın: play.
  Q5: My father ___ TV every evening. (watch) [watches / watchs / watch] -> watches :: -ch ile bitince -es: watch → watches.

=== 2-3 [grammar] Gramer: Olumsuz & Soru (don't / doesn't)
intro: Geniş zamanda "yapmıyorum" demek ve soru sormak için yardımcı kelime kullanırız.
rule: Olumsuz: I/you/we/they + <b>don't</b> + fiil. he/she/it + <b>doesn't</b> + fiil. Soru: <b>Do</b> you...? <b>Does</b> she...? (Bu durumda fiil hep yalındır.)
structure: [["Özne","Olumsuz","Soru"],["I/You/We/They","don't like","Do you like?"],["He/She/It","doesn't like","Does he like?"]]
  ex: I don't like coffee. = Kahveyi sevmem.
  ex: She doesn't eat meat. = O et yemez.
  ex: Do you speak English? = İngilizce konuşur musun?
  ex: Does he work here? = O burada mı çalışıyor?
  Q1: She ___ like tea. [doesn't / don't / not] -> doesn't :: He/She/It ile doesn't.
  Q2: I ___ eat fish. [don't / doesn't / not] -> don't :: I ile don't.
  Q3: ___ you speak English? [Do / Does / Are] -> Do :: you ile soru: Do you...?
  Q4: ___ he work here? [Does / Do / Is] -> Does :: he ile soru: Does he...?
  Q5: My brother ___ drink coffee. [doesn't / don't / isn't] -> doesn't :: 'My brother' = he → doesn't.
  Q6: ___ they live here? [Do / Does / Are] -> Do :: they ile soru: Do they...?
  Q7: Does she ___ tea? [like / likes / liking] -> like :: Does'tan sonra fiil yalın: like (likes değil).

=== 2-4 [grammar] Gramer: have / has (sahip olmak)

rule: I/You/We/They + <b>have</b>: I have a car. He/She/It + <b>has</b>: She has a cat.
structure: [["Özne","Fiil","Örnek"],["I / You / We / They","have","I have a car"],["He / She / It","has","She has a cat"]]
  ex: I have a car. = Bir arabam var.
  ex: She has two cats. = İki kedisi var.
  ex: We have a big house. = Büyük bir evimiz var.
  ex: He has a phone. = Onun telefonu var.
  Q1: I ___ a car. [have / has / haves] -> have :: I ile have.
  Q2: She ___ two cats. [has / have / haves] -> has :: She ile has.
  Q3: We ___ a big house. [have / has / having] -> have :: We ile have.
  Q4: He ___ a phone. [has / have / haves] -> has :: He ile has.
  Q5: My parents ___ a garden. [have / has / haves] -> have :: 'My parents' = they → have.
  Q6: The house ___ three rooms. [has / have / haves] -> has :: 'The house' = it → has.
  Q7: You ___ a beautiful smile. [have / has / having] -> have :: You ile have.

=== 2-6 [grammar] Gramer: can / can't (yapabilmek)
intro: Bir şeyi yapabildiğimizi veya yapamadığımızı anlatır.

=== 2-7 [grammar] Gramer: Edatlar (in / on / under / at)
intro: Bir şeyin nerede olduğunu anlatan küçük kelimeler.

  Q1: The book is ___ the table. (üstünde) [on / in / under] -> on :: üstünde = on.
  Q2: The cat is ___ the box. (içinde) [in / on / at] -> in :: içinde = in.
  Q3: The ball is ___ the chair. (altında) [under / on / in] -> under :: altında = under.
  Q4: I am ___ home. [at / in / on] -> at :: at home kalıptır.
  Q5: The keys are ___ my bag. (içinde) [in / on / under] -> in :: içinde = in.
  Q6: The shoes are ___ the bed. (altında) [under / on / at] -> under :: altında = under.
  Q7: The lesson starts ___ 9 o'clock. [at / in / on] -> at :: Saatlerle at kullanılır: at 9 o'clock.

=== 2-14 [grammar] Gramer: There is / There are (var / yok)
intro: Bir yerde bir şeyin "var" olduğunu söylemek için kullanılır. "Masada bir kitap var" gibi.
rule: Tekil için <b>There is</b> (kısaca There's): There is a book. Çoğul için <b>There are</b>: There are two cats. Olumsuz: <b>There isn't / There aren't</b>. Soru: <b>Is there...? / Are there...?</b>
structure: [["","Tekil","Çoğul"],["Olumlu","There is a bank","There are two banks"],["Olumsuz","There isn't a bank","There aren't any banks"],["Soru","Is there a bank?","Are there any banks?"]]
  ex: There is a cat in the garden. = Bahçede bir kedi var.
  ex: There are three rooms in the house. = Evde üç oda var.
  ex: There isn't a hospital in our village. = Köyümüzde hastane yok.
  ex: Is there a pharmacy near here? = Buralarda eczane var mı?
  Q1: There ___ a book on the table. [is / are / be] -> is :: Tekil (a book) → There is.
  Q2: There ___ two cats in the garden. [are / is / am] -> are :: Çoğul (two cats) → There are.
  Q3: ___ there a bank near here? [Is / Are / Do] -> Is :: Tekil soru: Is there...?
  Q4: ___ there any chairs in the room? [Are / Is / Do] -> Are :: Çoğul soru: Are there...?
  Q5: There ___ a hospital in our village. (yok) [isn't / aren't / not] -> isn't :: Tekil + olumsuz → There isn't.
  Q6: There ___ any eggs in the fridge. (yok) [aren't / isn't / not] -> aren't :: Çoğul + olumsuz → There aren't.
  Q7: There ___ many people at the bus stop. [are / is / be] -> are :: people çoğuldur → There are.

=== 2-15 [grammar] Gramer: Soru Kelimeleri (What, Where, When...)
intro: Bilgi istemek için soru kelimeleri kullanırız. Soru kelimesi cümlenin en başına gelir.
rule: <b>What</b> = ne, <b>Where</b> = nerede/nereye, <b>When</b> = ne zaman, <b>Who</b> = kim, <b>Why</b> = neden, <b>How</b> = nasıl, <b>How much</b> = ne kadar (fiyat). Örnek: "<b>Where</b> is the bank?" = "Banka <b>nerede</b>?"
structure: [["Soru kelimesi","Anlam","Örnek"],["What","ne","What is this?"],["Where","nerede","Where is the station?"],["When","ne zaman","When is the bus?"],["Who","kim","Who is he?"],["Why","neden","Why are you sad?"],["How","nasıl","How are you?"],["How much","ne kadar","How much is it?"]]
  ex: Where do you live? = Nerede yaşıyorsun?
  ex: When is your birthday? = Doğum günün ne zaman?
  ex: Who is that man? = Şu adam kim?
  ex: Why are you tired? = Neden yorgunsun?
  ex: How much is this bag? = Bu çanta ne kadar?
  Q1: ___ is the bus stop? — It's over there. [Where / When / Who] -> Where :: Yer soruyoruz → Where (nerede).
  Q2: ___ is your birthday? — In May. [When / Where / What] -> When :: Zaman soruyoruz → When (ne zaman).
  Q3: ___ is that woman? — She is my aunt. [Who / What / Where] -> Who :: Kişi soruyoruz → Who (kim).
  Q4: ___ are you sad? — Because I am tired. [Why / How / When] -> Why :: Sebep soruyoruz → Why (neden). Cevap 'Because' (çünkü) ile başlar.
  Q5: ___ is this? — It's a phone. [What / Who / Where] -> What :: Bir şeyi soruyoruz → What (ne).
  Q6: ___ are you? — I am fine, thanks. [How / What / Who] -> How :: Hâl hatır soruyoruz → How (nasıl).
  Q7: How ___ is this coffee? — Three pounds. [much / many / old] -> much :: Fiyat sorarken: How much (ne kadar).

=== 3-1 [grammar] Gramer: Şimdiki Zaman (Present Continuous)
intro: Tam şu anda yapılan işi anlatır. "Şu an okuyorum" gibi.

  ex: She is cooking now. = O şu an yemek yapıyor.
  ex: They are playing. = Onlar oynuyorlar.
  ex: We are working now. = Şu an çalışıyoruz.
  Q1: I ___ reading. [am / is / are] -> am :: I ile am + ...ing.
  Q2: She ___ cooking. [is / am / are] -> is :: She ile is + ...ing.
  Q3: They ___ playing. [are / is / am] -> are :: They ile are + ...ing.

  ex: She was a teacher. = O öğretmendi.
  ex: They were tired. = Onlar yorgundu.
  ex: We were happy. = Biz mutluyduk.

  Q2: They ___ tired. [were / was / are] -> were :: They ile were.
  Q3: She ___ a teacher. [was / were / is] -> was :: She ile was.
  Q4: We ___ happy. [were / was / are] -> were :: We ile were.
  Q5: You ___ late yesterday. [were / was / are] -> were :: You ile were.

=== 3-3 [grammar] Gramer: Geçmiş Zaman (-ed)
intro: Düzenli fiillerin geçmiş hali. Sona -ed eklenir.
rule: Düzenli fiil + <b>-ed</b>: play → play<b>ed</b>, work → work<b>ed</b>, like → lik<b>ed</b> (-e ile bitene sadece -d). "I played football" = "Futbol oynadım."

  ex: I played football. = Futbol oynadım.
  ex: She worked hard. = O çok çalıştı.
  ex: We watched a film. = Bir film izledik.
  ex: They liked the food. = Yemeği beğendiler.
  Q1: I ___ football yesterday. (play) [played / play / plaied] -> played :: play → played.
  Q2: She ___ hard. (work) [worked / work / worken] -> worked :: work → worked.
  Q3: We ___ a film. (watch) [watched / watch / watcheded] -> watched :: watch → watched.
  Q4: They ___ the food. (like) [liked / likeed / like] -> liked :: like → liked.
  Q5: I ___ my mother yesterday. (call) [called / call / calld] -> called :: call → called.
  Q6: We ___ the house last week. (clean) [cleaned / clean / cleant] -> cleaned :: clean → cleaned.
  Q7: She ___ in London for a year. (live) [lived / liveed / live] -> lived :: -e ile biten fiile sadece -d: live → lived.

=== 3-4 [grammar] Gramer: Düzensiz Fiiller
intro: Bazı fiiller geçmişte -ed almaz; tamamen değişir. Bunları ezberlemek gerekir.
rule: go → <b>went</b>, have → <b>had</b>, see → <b>saw</b>, eat → <b>ate</b>, come → <b>came</b>, do → <b>did</b>.
structure: [["Şimdi","Geçmiş","Anlam"],["go","went","gitmek"],["eat","ate","yemek"],["see","saw","görmek"],["have","had","sahip olmak"],["come","came","gelmek"]]
  ex: I went to school. = Okula gittim.
  ex: She ate an apple. = O bir elma yedi.
  ex: We saw a film. = Bir film izledik.
  ex: He had a car. = Onun arabası vardı.
  Q1: I ___ to school. (go) [went / goed / go] -> went :: go → went.
  Q2: She ___ an apple. (eat) [ate / eated / eat] -> ate :: eat → ate.
  Q3: We ___ a film. (see) [saw / seed / see] -> saw :: see → saw.
  Q4: He ___ a car. (have) [had / haved / has] -> had :: have → had.
  Q5: They ___ home late last night. (come) [came / comed / come] -> came :: come → came.
  Q6: I ___ my homework yesterday. (do) [did / doed / do] -> did :: do → did.
  Q7: We ___ to the sea last summer. (go) [went / goed / go] -> went :: go → went.

=== 3-5 [grammar] Gramer: Gelecek Zaman (will / going to)
intro: İleride olacak işleri anlatır. İki yol vardır.
rule: <b>will</b> + fiil = anlık karar, söz veya tahmin: I will help you. <b>be going to</b> + fiil = plan: I am going to travel.
structure: [["Yapı","Kullanım","Örnek"],["will + fiil","karar/söz/tahmin","It will rain"],["be going to + fiil","plan","I am going to study"]]
  ex: I will help you. = Sana yardım edeceğim.
  ex: It will rain. = Yağmur yağacak.
  ex: I am going to travel. = Seyahat edeceğim (planlı).
  ex: She is going to cook. = O yemek yapacak (planlı).
  Q1: I ___ help you. (söz veriyorum) [will / going / am] -> will :: Anlık karar/söz: will.
  Q2: It ___ rain. (tahmin) [will / going / is] -> will :: Tahmin: will.
  Q3: I am ___ to travel. (plan) [going / will / go] -> going :: Plan: be going to.
  Q4: She is going to ___. (cook) [cook / cooks / cooking] -> cook :: going to'dan sonra fiil yalın.
  Q5: We are going to ___ my aunt tomorrow. (visit) [visit / visits / visiting] -> visit :: going to'dan sonra fiil yalın.
  Q6: The phone is ringing. — I ___ answer it! (anlık karar) [will / am going / going] -> will :: Anlık karar: will.
  Q7: They ___ going to buy a new car. (plan) [are / will / is] -> are :: They ile be going to: are going to.

=== 3-6 [grammar] Gramer: Karşılaştırma (bigger, better)
intro: İki şeyi karşılaştırırken kullanılır. "daha büyük" gibi.
rule: Kısa sıfat + <b>-er</b> + <b>than</b>: big → big<b>ger</b> than. Uzun sıfatlarda <b>more</b>: more beautiful. İstisnalar: good → <b>better</b>, bad → <b>worse</b>.
structure: [["Sıfat","Karşılaştırma"],["big","bigger"],["small","smaller"],["old","older"],["good","better"],["bad","worse"]]
  ex: An elephant is bigger than a cat. = Fil kediden daha büyük.
  ex: This is better than that. = Bu şundan daha iyi.
  ex: A mouse is smaller than a dog. = Fare köpekten daha küçük.
  Q1: An elephant is ___ than a cat. (big) [bigger / biger / more big] -> bigger :: big → bigger (g iki kez).
  Q2: This is ___ than that. (good) [better / gooder / more good] -> better :: good → better (istisna).
  Q3: A mouse is ___ than a dog. (small) [smaller / smallier / more small] -> smaller :: small → smaller.
  Q4: Today is ___ than yesterday. (bad) [worse / badder / more bad] -> worse :: bad → worse (istisna).
  Q5: A car is ___ than a bicycle. (fast) [faster / more fast / fastest] -> faster :: Kısa sıfat + -er: fast → faster.
  Q6: This dress is ___ than that one. (beautiful) [more beautiful / beautifuler / most beautiful] -> more beautiful :: Uzun sıfatlarda more: more beautiful.
  Q7: My brother is older ___ me. [than / that / then] -> than :: Karşılaştırmada 'than' (-den/-dan) kullanılır.

=== 3-10 [grammar] Gramer: Geçmiş Zaman Olumsuz & Soru (didn't / Did...?)
intro: Geçmişte "yapmadım" demek ve soru sormak için did kullanırız. Geniş zamandaki do/does gibidir.
rule: Olumsuz: özne + <b>didn't</b> + fiil (yalın): I <b>didn't</b> go. Soru: <b>Did</b> + özne + fiil (yalın)?: <b>Did</b> you eat? Dikkat: did varken fiil geçmiş hale girmez. "I didn't went" yanlış, "I didn't <b>go</b>" doğru.
structure: [["Olumlu","Olumsuz","Soru"],["I went","I didn't go","Did you go?"],["She ate","She didn't eat","Did she eat?"],["They played","They didn't play","Did they play?"]]
  ex: I didn't sleep well. = İyi uyumadım.
  ex: She didn't come to work. = O işe gelmedi.
  ex: Did you see my glasses? = Gözlüğümü gördün mü?
  ex: Did they call you? = Seni aradılar mı?
  Q1: I ___ go to work yesterday. [didn't / don't / wasn't] -> didn't :: Geçmişte olumsuz: didn't + fiil.
  Q2: ___ you eat breakfast this morning? [Did / Do / Were] -> Did :: Geçmişte soru: Did you...?
  Q3: She didn't ___ the film. (like) [like / liked / likes] -> like :: didn't'ten sonra fiil yalın: like.
  Q4: Did he ___ you yesterday? (call) [call / called / calls] -> call :: Did ile fiil yalın kalır: call.
  Q5: We ___ see him at the party last night. [didn't / doesn't / weren't] -> didn't :: Geçmiş olumsuz: didn't see.
  Q6: Did you go to the market? — Yes, I ___. [did / went / do] -> did :: Kısa cevap: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
  Q7: They didn't ___ home early. (come) [come / came / comes] -> come :: didn't'ten sonra düzensiz fiil de yalın: come (came değil).

  Q4: My grandchildren are here. I play with ___. [them / they / their] -> them :: Edattan sonra nesne: they → them.
  Q5: Please call ___ tomorrow. (beni) [me / I / my] -> me :: I → me (beni/bana).
  Q6: Ali is my neighbour. I talk to ___ every day. [him / her / them] -> him :: Ali erkek → him.
  Q7: This cake is for ___. (senin için) [you / your / yours] -> you :: you hem özne hem nesne olarak aynıdır: for you.

##### LEVEL 4 B1 Seviye 4 — Orta

=== 4-1 [grammar] Gramer: Present Perfect (have/has + V3)
intro: Geçmişte olmuş ama etkisi şimdi de süren olayları ya da hayat tecrübelerini anlatır. "Hiç Londra'ya gittin mi?" gibi.
rule: <b>have/has</b> + fiilin 3. hali (V3). Düzenli fiillerde V3 = -ed: work → work<b>ed</b>. Düzensizler ezberlenir: go → <b>gone</b>, see → <b>seen</b>, eat → <b>eaten</b>, be → <b>been</b>. <b>ever</b> = hiç (soruda), <b>never</b> = hiç (olumsuz anlam), <b>already</b> = çoktan, <b>yet</b> = henüz.
structure: [["Özne","Yapı","Örnek"],["I/You/We/They","have + V3","I have finished"],["He/She/It","has + V3","She has gone"],["Soru","Have/Has ... + V3?","Have you ever been to Rome?"],["Olumsuz","haven't/hasn't + V3","He hasn't called yet"]]
  ex: I have lost my keys. = Anahtarlarımı kaybettim (hâlâ yoklar).
  ex: She has never seen snow. = O hiç kar görmedi.
  ex: Have you ever been to London? = Hiç Londra'da bulundun mu?
  ex: We have already eaten. = Biz çoktan yedik.
  ex: He hasn't called yet. = O henüz aramadı.
  Q1: I ___ finished my work. [have / has / am] -> have :: I ile have + V3.
  Q2: She ___ gone to the market. [has / have / is] -> has :: She ile has + V3.
  Q3: Have you ever ___ to Paris? (be) [been / was / be] -> been :: be fiilinin 3. hali: been.
  Q4: We have ___ this film before. (see) [seen / saw / see] -> seen :: see → saw → seen. Present perfect'te 3. hal: seen.
  Q5: He hasn't called ___. [yet / already / ever] -> yet :: Olumsuz cümlenin sonunda 'yet' (henüz).
  Q6: I have ___ eaten sushi. (hiç) [never / yet / ever] -> never :: Olumlu cümlede 'hiç' anlamı için never: I have never eaten...
  Q7: They have ___ left. The house is empty. (çoktan) [already / yet / never] -> already :: çoktan = already.

=== 4-3 [grammar] Gramer: Geçmişte Süren Zaman (was/were + -ing)
intro: Geçmişte belli bir anda devam eden işi anlatır. "Dün akşam saat 8'de yemek yapıyordum" gibi.
rule: <b>was/were</b> + fiil + <b>-ing</b>. I/he/she/it + <b>was</b>, you/we/they + <b>were</b>. Süren bir iş kısa bir olayla kesilince: <b>when</b> + basit geçmiş zaman: I was cooking <b>when</b> the phone rang. İki iş aynı anda sürüyorsa: <b>while</b>.
structure: [["Özne","Yapı","Örnek"],["I/He/She/It","was + ...ing","I was sleeping"],["You/We/They","were + ...ing","They were talking"],["Olumsuz","wasn't/weren't + ...ing","She wasn't working"],["Soru","Was/Were ... + ...ing?","Were you sleeping?"]]
  ex: I was cooking at 8 o'clock. = Saat 8'de yemek yapıyordum.
  ex: They were watching TV. = Onlar televizyon izliyorlardı.
  ex: I was sleeping when the phone rang. = Telefon çaldığında uyuyordum.
  ex: She was reading while he was cooking. = Adam yemek yaparken kadın kitap okuyordu.
  Q1: I ___ sleeping at midnight last night. [was / were / am] -> was :: I ile was + ...ing.
  Q2: They ___ playing in the garden at 5 pm yesterday. [were / was / are] -> were :: They ile were + ...ing (geçmiş zaman: yesterday).
  Q3: She was ___ a book. (read) [reading / read / reads] -> reading :: was + fiil + -ing: reading.
  Q4: I was cooking when the phone ___. [rang / was ringing / rings] -> rang :: Araya giren kısa olay basit geçmiş zamanla anlatılır: rang.
  Q5: We ___ watching TV when you called. [were / was / are] -> were :: We ile were + ...ing.
  Q6: ___ you sleeping when I called? [Were / Was / Did] -> Were :: you ile soru: Were you ...ing?
  Q7: He ___ working yesterday. He was at home. (değildi) [wasn't / weren't / didn't] -> wasn't :: He ile olumsuz: wasn't + ...ing.

info: Dükkân adlarında 's genelde 'dükkânı' anlamına gelir: 'the butcher's' = kasap dükkânı, 'the dry cleaner's' = kuru temizlemeci. 'I'm going to the bank.'

=== 4-5 [grammar] Gramer: If (Birinci Koşul Cümlesi)
intro: Gerçekleşmesi mümkün bir koşulu ve sonucunu anlatır. "Yağmur yağarsa evde kalırız" gibi.
rule: <b>If</b> + geniş zaman, <b>will</b> + fiil: If it rain<b>s</b>, we <b>will</b> stay at home. Dikkat: if'li kısımda will kullanılmaz; "If it will rain" yanlıştır. Kısımların yeri değişebilir: We will stay at home if it rains.
structure: [["If kısmı (koşul)","Sonuç kısmı"],["If it rains,","we will stay at home."],["If you study,","you will pass."],["If she is tired,","she won't come."]]
  ex: If it rains, I will take an umbrella. = Yağmur yağarsa şemsiye alırım.
  ex: If you are hungry, I will cook. = Açsan yemek yaparım.
  ex: If we hurry, we won't miss the bus. = Acele edersek otobüsü kaçırmayız.
  ex: I will call you if I am late. = Geç kalırsam seni ararım.
  Q1: If it ___, we will stay at home. (rain) [rains / will rain / rained] -> rains :: If kısmında geniş zaman: rains.
  Q2: If you study, you ___ pass the exam. [will / are / did] -> will :: Sonuç kısmında will + fiil.
  Q3: If I ___ tired, I will go to bed early. [am / will be / was] -> am :: If kısmında will kullanılmaz: If I am tired.
  Q4: She will ___ you if she has time. (help) [help / helps / helped] -> help :: will'den sonra fiil yalın: help.
  Q5: If we don't hurry, we ___ miss the bus. [will / do / are] -> will :: Sonuç kısmı: will miss.
  Q6: If he ___ the bus, he will be late. (miss) [misses / will miss / miss] -> misses :: If kısmında geniş zaman; he ile -es: misses.
  Q7: If it is cold, I ___ go out. (çıkmayacağım) [won't / don't will / am not] -> won't :: will not = won't. Sonuç kısmında olumsuz: won't + fiil.

=== 4-6 [vocab] Haberler & Fikirler
desc: Haberleri konuşmak ve fikrini söylemek.
  1. News = Haberler | I watch the news every evening. | Her akşam haberleri izlerim.
  2. Newspaper = Gazete | My father reads the newspaper. | Babam gazete okur.
  3. Headline = Manşet | Did you see the headline today? | Bugünkü manşeti gördün mü?
  4. Article = Makale / Yazı | This article is very interesting. | Bu yazı çok ilginç.
  5. Weather forecast = Hava tahmini | The weather forecast says it will rain. | Hava tahminine göre yağmur yağacak.
  6. In my opinion = Bana göre / Bence | In my opinion, it is a good idea. | Bence bu iyi bir fikir.
  7. I think so = Sanırım öyle | Is it true? — I think so. | Doğru mu? — Sanırım öyle.
  8. I agree = Katılıyorum | I agree with you. | Sana katılıyorum.
  9. I disagree = Katılmıyorum | Sorry, I disagree. | Kusura bakma, katılmıyorum.
  10. That's true = Bu doğru | That's true. You are right. | Bu doğru. Haklısın.
  11. Really? = Gerçekten mi? | Really? I didn't know that. | Gerçekten mi? Bunu bilmiyordum.
  12. Maybe = Belki | Maybe you are right. | Belki haklısın.
info: Fikir bildirmek: 'In my opinion ...' ya da kısaca 'I think ...'. Katılmak 'I agree', katılmamak 'I disagree'. Şaşırınca 'Really?' denir.

=== 4-7 [grammar] Gramer: should / must / have to
intro: Tavsiye vermek ve zorunluluk anlatmak için kullanılır.
rule: <b>should</b> + fiil = tavsiye (-meli, iyi olur): You <b>should</b> rest. <b>must</b> + fiil = güçlü zorunluluk, kural: You <b>must</b> stop at a red light. <b>have to / has to</b> + fiil = gereklilik: I <b>have to</b> work tomorrow. <b>mustn't</b> = yasak! <b>don't have to</b> = gerek yok.
structure: [["Yapı","Anlam","Örnek"],["should","tavsiye","You should see a doctor"],["must","zorunluluk / kural","You must wear a seatbelt"],["have to / has to","gereklilik","She has to work on Saturday"],["mustn't","yasak","You mustn't smoke here"],["don't have to","gerek yok","You don't have to pay"]]
  ex: You should drink more water. = Daha çok su içmelisin.
  ex: You must show your passport. = Pasaportunu göstermek zorundasın.
  ex: I have to go to the bank. = Bankaya gitmem gerekiyor.
  ex: You mustn't park here. = Buraya park etmek yasak.
  ex: You don't have to come. = Gelmek zorunda değilsin.
  Q1: You look tired. You ___ rest. (tavsiye) [should / mustn't / has to] -> should :: Tavsiye: should + fiil.
  Q2: She ___ work on Saturdays. [has to / have to / must to] -> has to :: she ile has to. (must'tan sonra 'to' gelmez.)
  Q3: You ___ smoke in the hospital. (yasak) [mustn't / don't have to / should] -> mustn't :: Yasak: mustn't.
  Q4: It's Sunday. I ___ get up early. (gerek yok) [don't have to / mustn't / must] -> don't have to :: Gerek yok: don't have to.
  Q5: You must ___ your seatbelt. (wear) [wear / to wear / wearing] -> wear :: must'tan sonra fiil yalın gelir: must wear.
  Q6: I have a toothache. I ___ see a dentist. (tavsiye) [should / shouldn't / don't have to] -> should :: Tavsiye: should.
  Q7: We ___ be at the airport at 6. Our flight is at 8. [have to / has to / must to] -> have to :: we ile have to.

=== 4-8 [vocab] Ev İşleri
desc: Ev işleri ve küçük tamirler.
  1. Do the laundry = Çamaşır yıkamak | I do the laundry on Mondays. | Pazartesileri çamaşır yıkarım.
  2. Wash the dishes = Bulaşık yıkamak | Can you wash the dishes? | Bulaşıkları yıkayabilir misin?
  3. Clean = Temizlemek | I clean the kitchen every day. | Her gün mutfağı temizlerim.
  4. Iron = Ütülemek | I iron the shirts. | Gömlekleri ütülerim.
  5. Vacuum = Süpürge çekmek | I vacuum the living room. | Oturma odasına süpürge çekerim.
  6. Tidy up = Ortalığı toplamak | Please tidy up your room. | Lütfen odanı topla.
  7. Make the bed = Yatağı toplamak | I make the bed every morning. | Her sabah yatağı toplarım.
  8. Take out the rubbish = Çöpü çıkarmak | Can you take out the rubbish? | Çöpü çıkarabilir misin?
  9. Water the plants = Çiçekleri sulamak | I water the plants in the evening. | Akşamları çiçekleri sularım.
  10. Do the shopping = Alışveriş yapmak | We do the shopping on Saturday. | Cumartesi alışveriş yaparız.
  11. Broken = Bozuk / Kırık | The washing machine is broken. | Çamaşır makinesi bozuk.
  12. Repair = Tamir etmek | Can you repair the door? | Kapıyı tamir edebilir misin?
info: Ev işlerinde 'do' ve 'make' karışır: 'do the laundry', 'do the shopping' ama 'make the bed'. Bir şey bozuksa: 'It's broken. Can you repair it?'

=== 4-9 [grammar] Gramer: who / which (ilgi cümleleri)
intro: İki cümleyi birleştirip bir kişiyi ya da şeyi daha ayrıntılı anlatmamızı sağlar. "Yan tarafta oturan kadın" gibi.
rule: İnsanlar için <b>who</b>, eşyalar ve hayvanlar için <b>which</b> kullanılır. Günlük konuşmada ikisinin yerine <b>that</b> da olur. The woman <b>who</b> lives next door = yan tarafta yaşayan kadın. The bus <b>which</b> goes to the city = şehre giden otobüs.
structure: [["Kim/ne için","Kelime","Örnek"],["insan","who","the man who works here"],["eşya / hayvan","which","the car which is red"],["ikisi de","that","the book that I read"]]
  ex: The woman who lives next door is a nurse. = Yan tarafta yaşayan kadın hemşire.
  ex: This is the bus which goes to the airport. = Bu, havalimanına giden otobüs.
  ex: I have a friend who speaks Italian. = İtalyanca konuşan bir arkadaşım var.
  ex: Where is the cake which I made? = Yaptığım pasta nerede?
  Q1: The man ___ lives next door is a doctor. [who / which / where] -> who :: İnsan için who.
  Q2: This is the bus ___ goes to the city centre. [which / who / where] -> which :: Eşya (otobüs) için which.
  Q3: I have a friend ___ speaks English. [who / which / what] -> who :: İnsan (arkadaş) için who.
  Q4: The cake ___ you made is delicious. [which / who / what] -> which :: Eşya (pasta) için which.
  Q5: The nurse ___ helped me was very kind. [who / which / what] -> who :: İnsan (hemşire) için who.
  Q6: I lost the bag ___ my daughter gave me. [which / who / what] -> which :: Eşya (çanta) için which.
  Q7: The shop ___ sells fresh bread is closed today. [which / who / what] -> which :: Yer/eşya (dükkân) için which.
